# WaterNPCs character sprites

- **Source pack:** WaterNPCs.zip (added by the team; original author unknown)
- **Sheets:**
  - `WaterNPCs_Animations.png` — walk animations, 18×26 px frames, 12×16 grid.
    Each character = 3 columns × 4 rows (3 walk frames × directions
    down / left / right / up). 16 characters total (4×4 blocks).
  - `WaterNPCs.png` — static poses/props.
  - `WaterNPCs_Trainers.png` — large portrait sprites.

## License warning

These are Pokémon Gen-3-style sprites (Sailor / Swimmer / Fisherman trainer
classes). If they are ripped or derived from Nintendo assets, they are fine
for a jam prototype but NOT safe for commercial release or storefront pages.
