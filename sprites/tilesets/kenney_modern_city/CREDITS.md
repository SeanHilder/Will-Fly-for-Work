## Files

- `tilemap_packed.png` — the tile atlas: 16×16 px tiles, 37×28 grid (592×448 px), **no spacing** — use this for Godot TileSet atlases
- `sample_preview.png` — pack preview, for reference only (not used in-game)

The pack's individual `Tiles/*.png` files (1036 of them) were intentionally not
imported — the atlas covers all of them without generating thousands of
Godot .import/.uid files.
